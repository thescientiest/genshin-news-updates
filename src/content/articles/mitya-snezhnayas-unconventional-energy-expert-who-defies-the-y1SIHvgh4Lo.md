## Summary

A new character spotlight introduces Mitya, a highly efficient specialist within Snezhnaya's Royal Energy Commission. Despite his crucial role, Mitya openly challenges conventional work attendance and expectations, believing that true inspiration and good work stem from a life well-lived and enjoyed, rather than being confined to a desk.

Mitya's unique philosophy sees him frequently exploring Snezhnograd, engaging in leisure activities, and even submitting unconventional proposals alongside his research reports. This cavalier approach to his schedule and his arguments for personal freedom ultimately caught the attention of his superiors, leading to "assistance" from a major, suggesting his methods have been acknowledged, if not entirely approved.

## Key Takeaways

- Mitya is a specialist in the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya.
- He prioritizes personal leisure and life experiences, believing they foster inspiration for his work.
- Mitya openly challenges traditional work attendance and expectations within Snezhnayan institutions.
- His unconventional methods and proposals have garnered the attention of his superiors.

This introduction provides a fascinating glimpse into the internal workings and societal norms of Snezhnaya beyond the familiar presence of the Fatui.
