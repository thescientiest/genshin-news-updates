## Summary

New details have emerged regarding Major Valeriy, the formidable commander of Snezhnaya's Special Assignments Battalion. Tasked with safeguarding the critical Armory Palace in Glupov, Valeriy leads an elite force dedicated to protecting the nation's foremost research, design, and production complex. This institution is responsible for developing cutting-edge technologies and military weaponry, holding secrets and assets of immeasurable value to Snezhnaya.

Major Valeriy's service record is highly distinguished, showcasing his prowess in both combat and administration. His battalion has successfully neutralized numerous threats, from monster concentrations linked to Ley Line disturbances and Volkodlak gangs to chimeric monsters and various attempts to infiltrate or sabotage the Armory Palace. Despite his rigorous duties, recent observations note Valeriy's frequent visits to entertainment venues, a topic he declines to address, preferring to let his impeccable work ethic speak for itself.

## Key Takeaways

- Major Valeriy commands the Special Assignments Battalion, an elite guard force in Snezhnaya.
- The Armory Palace in Glupov is Snezhnaya's primary research and production complex for advanced military technology and weapons.
- Valeriy's battalion protects the Armory Palace, having neutralized over ninety threats including monsters, bandits, and outlaws.
- Major Valeriy is noted for his exceptional combat skills, administrative precision, and meticulous scheduling, operating without an adjutant.
- Despite his demanding role, Valeriy has been observed frequenting entertainment venues, though he offers no comment on the matter.

## What This Means for Players

This new information offers a fascinating glimpse into the internal workings and military might of Snezhnaya, particularly highlighting the importance of the Armory Palace as a hub for technological advancement and defense. Major Valeriy's character profile suggests the presence of highly organized and capable military personnel beyond the Fatui Harbingers, hinting at the depth and complexity of the Cryo Archon's nation. For players, this deepens the lore surrounding Snezhnaya's infrastructure and military capabilities, potentially setting the stage for future story developments or character introductions as the Traveler eventually journeys to the snowbound nation.
