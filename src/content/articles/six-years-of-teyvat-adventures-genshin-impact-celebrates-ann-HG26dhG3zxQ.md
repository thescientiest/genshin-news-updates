Genshin Impact marks its 6th anniversary with a commemorative video and a heartfelt letter to its Travelers. The message reflects on a remarkable six-year journey across Teyvat, from the familiar breezes of Mondstadt to the blazing Sacred Flame of Natlan, and further into the moonlit northern regions. Over this period, players have collectively experienced countless adventures, followed guiding lights, and shared profound moments, including significant events and emotional milestones.

The anniversary letter emphasizes the ever-expanding world of Teyvat and the promise of new stories yet to unfold. It serves as a warm invitation for players to continue their journey alongside HoYoverse in the years to come, celebrating the enduring connection between the game and its dedicated community.
